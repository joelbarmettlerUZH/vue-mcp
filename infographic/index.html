Ingestion · Principle 04

Summaries,
same vector space.

Vague queries hit a top-level summary. Specific queries hit a leaf chunk. One retrieval call returns both.

L3 · Top summaries
~3 chunks
L2 · Folder summaries
~10 chunks
L1 · Page summaries
~50 chunks

~500–2000 chunks

Combine both retrievals. Fuse them with RRF inside Qdrant.

Retrieval · The thesis

Your link graph
IS retrieval signal.

Generic RAG ignores cross-references. Structure-aware RAG treats them as a domain-specific signal you mined for free at indexing time.

Retrieval · Principle 07

Reorder by position.

The reranker tells you which chunks are relevant. The sort key tells you which order they belong in. Use both.

Results · Recap · Resources

The point isn't the
stack. It's the structure.

Five principles for RAG that respects the shape of your data.

  1. Chunk at structural boundaries
  2. Embed each chunk in context
  3. Generate hypothetical questions
  4. Combine dense + sparse retrieval
  5. Treat your link graph as signal
